Blood Cells & Function

🔴 Red Blood Cells (Erythrocytes):

Contain hemoglobin to transport oxygen. Biconcave shape increases surface area; lacks nucleus for max space.

🛡️ White Blood Cells (Leukocytes):

Phagocytes: Engulf and digest microbes.
Lymphocytes: Produce Y-shaped antibodies that lock onto pathogen antigens.

⚡ Platelets & Plasma:

Platelets form blood clots at cuts. Plasma is the liquid transporting nutrients, $CO_2$, and urea.

Solubility Principles

Solute: The solid being dissolved (e.g., Sugar or Salt).
Solvent: The liquid doing the dissolving (e.g., Water).
Solution: Homogeneous mixture formed when solute dissolves in solvent.

🔥 Effect of Heat: High temperature endows water particles with kinetic energy. Faster collisions break solute bonds rapidly, increasing solubility capacity!

Chemical Equation
